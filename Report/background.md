# Trends in Incidence, Prevalence, and Survival of Breast Cancer in Individuals Under 50 in the UK

## A Population Based Cohort Study

This dashboard presents results from a population-based cohort study examining 
trends in breast cancer among individuals aged 18-49 years in the United
Kingdom. Using routinely collected electronic health records from the Clinical
Practice Research Datalink (CPRD) GOLD, we estimated incidence, prevalence, and
survival probability from 2000 to 2024. Breast cancer diagnoses were identified
using validated SNOMED CT codes, and survival was calculated from the date of
diagnosis to death using the Kaplan-Meier method. Results are summarised by age
group, region, and calendar year to highlight patterns in disease burden and
survival outcomes over time.
